Our History

Our school was established on June 15, 2015, by Yogendra Singh in memory of his father, the late Shri Tej Pratap Singh. The school was founded with the vision of fulfilling his father's dream of providing educational opportunities for underprivileged children in the area. At present, it stands as the first fully equipped English medium school in the region, educating 1000 + students.

The school boasts a remarkable academic record, with nearly 100% of students passing board exams and achieving top results in the area. Apart from academics, students actively participate in sports and cultural events, while the school organises grand programmes that uplift the student's spirits. Continuously striving to enhance facilities, the school is dedicated to providing the best for its students.
