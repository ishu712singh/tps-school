Our History

Founded in 1995, TPS Hinauta School has been a cornerstone of quality education in our community for over 25 years. What started as a small institution with just 50 students has grown into a comprehensive educational facility serving hundreds of families.

Throughout our history, we have remained committed to providing innovative educational experiences that prepare students for success in an ever-changing world. Our dedicated faculty and staff continue to uphold the traditions of excellence that have defined our school since its inception.
